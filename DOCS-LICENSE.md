## Why we care

Our documentation is written to help users make the most effective use of our published open-source programs. We strongly and warmly encourage contributions to the project in the form of pull requests (see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)), and accepted changes are suitably documented. Forks of our project are presumed to yield programs that behave or appear differently, and a modified copy of our documentation can describe protocol behavior, settings, or procedures that no longer match what such a fork actually does — misleading operators even beyond misrepresentation of authorship. Maintainers of forks should document them independently, without implying any endorsement or association with builds we haven't reviewed, don't control, and don't support.
